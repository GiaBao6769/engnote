

# Environment

## 1. The Natural Environment and Ecosystems

### Vocabulary

- **environment** (n/C-U): the natural world and the conditions in which people, animals and plants live
- **natural environment** (n/C): the physical world that exists without human creation
- **ecosystem** (n/C): a community of organisms interacting with their surroundings
- **habitat** (n/C): the natural home of a plant or animal
- **biodiversity** (n/U): the variety of plant and animal species in an area
- **wildlife** (n/U): animals and plants living in natural conditions
- **flora** (n/U): the plants found in a particular region
- **fauna** (n/U): the animals found in a particular region
- **vegetation** (n/U): the plants growing in a particular area
- **species** (n/C): a group of similar organisms capable of reproducing
- **organism** (n/C): an individual living thing
- **food chain** (n/C): a sequence showing how organisms obtain food from one another
- **food web** (n/C): a network of interconnected food chains
- **ecological balance** (n/U): a stable relationship between organisms and their environment
- **natural resources** (n/C): materials obtained from nature and used by humans
- **renewable resource** (n/C): a natural resource that can be replenished
- **non-renewable resource** (n/C): a resource that cannot be replaced within a human timescale
- **ecosystem services** (n/C): benefits that humans obtain from natural ecosystems
- **conservation** (n/U): the protection and careful management of natural resources

- **ecological** (adj): relating to the relationships between organisms and their environment
- **devastating** (adj): causing extensive destruction or damage
- **irreversible** (adj): impossible to change back or undo
- **carbon-intensive** (adj): producing large amounts of carbon emissions
- **energy-efficient** (adj): using relatively little energy to perform a task
- **resource-intensive** (adj): requiring large amounts of resources
- **climate-resilient** (adj): able to withstand and recover from climate-related effects

### Adverbs

- **sustainably** (adv): in a way that avoids exhausting natural resources
- **environmentally** (adv): in a way relating to the natural environment
- **significantly** (adv): to a considerable extent
- **drastically** (adv): to a very large or severe extent
- **irreversibly** (adv): in a way that cannot be reversed
- **increasingly** (adv): more and more over time
- **collectively** (adv): through the combined actions of a group
- **predominantly** (adv): mainly or mostly
- **excessively** (adv): to an unnecessarily or unreasonably great extent
- **efficiently** (adv): in a way that avoids wasting resources
