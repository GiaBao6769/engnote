# 1. General Health and Well-being

## Vocabulary

**health** (n/U): the condition of a person's physical and mental well-being

**healthcare** (n/U): the organised provision of medical services to individuals and communities

**well-being** (n/U): the state of being comfortable, healthy, and happy

**physical health** (n/U): the condition of the body and its ability to function properly

**mental health** (n/U): a person's psychological and emotional well-being

**overall health** (n/U): a person's general physical and mental condition

**life expectancy** (n/U): the average number of years a person is expected to live

**quality of life** (n/U): the general level of health, comfort, and satisfaction experienced by an individual

**health condition** (n/C): a particular state of physical or mental health

**chronic disease** (n/C): a medical condition that persists for a long time and often requires ongoing treatment

**acute illness** (n/C): a medical condition that develops suddenly and usually lasts for a relatively short period

**life-threatening** (adj): likely to cause death

**life-altering** (adj): having a major and lasting effect on someone's life

**health-conscious** (adj): careful about maintaining good health

**vulnerable** (adj): more likely than others to be harmed or affected by illness

# 2. Diseases and Medical Conditions

## Vocabulary

**disease** (n/C): an illness that affects the body or mind and disrupts normal functioning

**illness** (n/C-U): a condition in which someone is unwell

**infection** (n/C-U): a disease caused by harmful microorganisms entering the body

**virus** (n/C): a microscopic infectious agent that reproduces inside living cells

**bacterial infection** (n/C): an illness caused by harmful bacteria

**epidemic** (n/C): a sudden increase in the number of cases of a disease within a particular area

**pandemic** (n/C): an epidemic that spreads across multiple countries or continents

**outbreak** (n/C): the sudden occurrence of a disease in a particular community or region

**contagious** (adj): capable of spreading from one person to another through contact or other means

**infectious** (adj): capable of causing or spreading infection

**communicable disease** (n/C): a disease that can spread between people or from animals to humans

**non-communicable disease** (n/C): a disease that cannot normally be transmitted between people

**cardiovascular disease** (n/C): a condition affecting the heart or blood vessels

**diabetes** (n/U): a chronic condition in which the body cannot properly regulate blood glucose levels

**obesity** (n/U): a medical condition involving excessive body fat that may harm health

**malnutrition** (n/U): poor health caused by an insufficient or unbalanced intake of nutrients

**allergy** (n/C): an abnormal immune reaction to a substance that is usually harmless

**disability** (n/C-U): a physical or mental condition that limits a person's activities or participation in everyday life

**symptom** (n/C): a physical or mental change that indicates the presence of an illness

**complication** (n/C): an additional medical problem that develops during or after an illness or treatment
