# 1. General Technology and Scientific Development

## Vocabulary
**technology** (n/C-U): the use of scientific knowledge to develop tools, machines, and systems

**technological advancement** (n/C): a significant improvement in technology

**innovation** (n/C-U): a new idea, method, or product that improves existing practices

**invention** (n/C): a new device, method, or process created through research or experimentation

**breakthrough** (n/C): an important discovery or development that leads to significant progress

**scientific research** (n/U): systematic investigation conducted to discover new knowledge

**scientific discovery** (n/C): a new finding that improves our understanding of the world

**research and development** (n/U): activities aimed at creating new products, technologies, and knowledge

**cutting-edge technology** (n/U): the most advanced technology currently available

**state-of-the-art** (adj): using the most modern and advanced technology

**obsolete** (adj): no longer useful or relevant because something newer exists

**conventional** (adj): based on traditional methods rather than modern developments

**emerging technology** (n/C): a technology that is relatively new and still developing

**automation** (n/U): the use of machines or computer systems to perform tasks with little human involvement

**digital transformation** (n/U): the process of integrating digital technology into different areas of society

## Collocations
- to drive technological advancement
- to promote scientific innovation
- to conduct scientific research
- to develop new technologies
- to introduce innovative solutions
- to invest in research and development
- to accelerate technological progress
- to keep pace with technological change
- technological breakthroughs (nP/C)
- scientific knowledge (nP/U)
- cutting-edge technology (nP/U)
- emerging technologies (nP/C)
- research and development (nP/U)
- technological infrastructure (nP/U)

# 2. Artificial Intelligence and Automation

## Vocabulary

**robotics** (n/U): the science and technology of designing and operating robots

**robot** (n/C): a machine capable of carrying out tasks automatically

**facial recognition** (n/U): technology that identifies people by analysing their facial features

**human oversight** (n/U): supervision by people to ensure that automated systems operate appropriately

**job displacement** (n/U): the loss of employment when workers are replaced by technology or other changes

**human-machine interaction** (n/U): communication and cooperation between people and machines

# 4. Privacy and Cybersecurity

## Vocabulary
**privacy** (n/U): the right to keep personal information and activities private

**data privacy** (n/U): the protection of personal information from unauthorised access or disclosure

**cybersecurity** (n/U): the practice of protecting computer systems, networks, and data from digital attacks

**identity theft** (n/U): the illegal use of another person's personal information

**hacking** (n/U): the unauthorised access to computer systems or networks

**encryption** (n/U): the process of converting information into a coded form to prevent unauthorised access

**surveillance** (n/U): the close observation or monitoring of people and their activities

**consent** (n/U): permission given for something to happen or for information to be used

**vulnerability** (n/C-U): a weakness that can be exploited or cause harm

**confidential** (adj): intended to be kept private or shared only with authorised people

**susceptible** (adj): easily affected or harmed by something
